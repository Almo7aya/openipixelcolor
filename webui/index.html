Text

Up to 4 messages play one after another, each with its own way in, out and in between. Any language works; Arabic joins up and runs right to left. With the panel font, {time} {secs} {date} {day} {year} show live values.

The panel font only has English letters, digits and signs. Pick another font for other scripts.
